Working with me

What you can expect from me

Less about tools, more about how I show up on a team.

I plan, then I ship

I start each day with clear priorities and finish things. My face recognition system serves paying clients at under 100ms because I kept going past the demo.

I measure honestly

I don't trust a model because it looks good once. I test against edge cases, track what changes, and tell you plainly when a number is not good enough.

I'm steady when it's hard

Lifting taught me to stay calm under load. SkyFusion had no clean baseline to copy, so I built one step by step and cut forecast error by 34.1%.

I say what I know and what I don't

I ask direct questions early, explain my reasoning in plain language, and bring teammates along instead of handing over a black box.
